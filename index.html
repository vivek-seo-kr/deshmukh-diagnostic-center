Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What documents are required for an X-ray or Ultrasound?

Please bring your doctor's prescription and a valid government-issued ID proof for registration.

Q. Is any preparation needed for an Ultrasound?

For abdominal ultrasounds, you may need to come with a full bladder or fast for a few hours. Please call us to confirm specific instructions for your scan.

Q. How long does it take to get radiology reports?

Most routine X-ray and Ultrasound reports are available within 2-4 hours. Digital copies can be sent immediately.

Q. Do you offer portable X-ray services?

Currently, we provide all services at our center to ensure the highest image quality and safety standards.

Q. Are you open on Sundays?

We are closed on Sundays. Our regular working hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
